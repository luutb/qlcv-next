# requirements-analysis

1. State actor, goal, current/expected behavior and exclusions; cite known business rules.
2. Identify inputs/entities, permissions, constraints, non-functional needs, dependencies and failure/boundary cases.
3. Assign AC IDs with observable given/when/then outcomes. Separate facts, assumptions and decisions requiring stakeholder input; never invent business policy.
4. Resolve material ambiguity or document a blocker; prioritize with product authority. Use the [requirements template](../../../docs/ai-development/templates/requirements.md) only when persistent handoff is useful.
Complete when scope and AC can drive implementation and verification; no PRD for an isolated bug.
